# Source-labelled skin phototype examples

Retrieved 13 September 2026. These are educational illustrations, not a visual diagnostic tool. The clinical sources explicitly identify the phototype; no type was inferred from appearance. Original image files are reproduced without cropping, recolouring or other alteration. CSS contains the complete image, including watermarks.

Phototype describes the response to sun exposure. Lighting, body area and clinical conditions affect appearance. The Type VI torso shows vitiligo; the cheek is a clinical photograph after treatment for dyschromia. These conditions are disclosed on the card and are not defining features of phototype VI. The article is cited for its explicit phototype label and image, not as evidence for a treatment recommendation.
